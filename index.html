Ongoing Hackathon 6.0 — two rounds

Featured 02 — Independent Research

Healthcare Access & Marketing Research

RUHS Hospital, Jaipur — whether a low-cost, evidence-based outreach campaign could improve informed hospital choice among underserved communities.

Independent project Python · Geospatial analysis 2026
Settlements mapped
50+
Hypotheses tested
8
Research report
20,000 words
Pilot budget ceiling
₹10,000
  1. 01

    Problem

    RUHS is a newer public hospital in Jaipur; SMS is the older and far busier one. The starting assumption was that nearby low-income settlements simply did not know RUHS existed.

  2. 02

    Research

    Reviewed academic literature on hospital choice, national survey data on why people avoid public facilities, and healthcare outreach case studies including Aravind Eye Care.

  3. 03

    Analysis

    Built a geospatial dataset of 50+ settlements using Python, coordinate mapping and Haversine distance calculations, then scored each one for socioeconomic need — with every figure carrying a source, and gaps marked rather than estimated.

  4. 04

    Hypotheses

    Tested eight explanations for hospital choice using the Health Belief Model and Theory of Planned Behaviour. The evidence did not support the original awareness-gap assumption — so I reframed the problem instead of defending it.

  5. 05

    Strategy

    Designed a trusted-intermediary campaign and a quasi-experimental pilot within a ₹10,000 budget, including sample-size and power calculations and a pre-committed rule for dropping the campaign if the diagnosis proved wrong.
