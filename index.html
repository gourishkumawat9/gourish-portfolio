Experience

Youth4Water Plus

Research Intern — Operation & Maintenance (O&M) Models for Multi-Village Piped Water Supply Schemes, Odisha

Part of a UNICEF-supported Youth for Water Project, implemented by BGVS Odisha

My contribution was primarily research and policy analysis, feeding into a broader team effort on how multi-village water schemes stay operational and financially sustainable after handover.

  • Reviewed Jal Jeevan Mission resources and guidelines, and Odisha's O&M framework
  • Examined institutional responsibilities across scheme stakeholders
  • Ran a comparative analysis of Indian and international O&M models
  • Analysed physical, financial, operational and institutional sustainability
  • Studied long-term O&M and EPC handover issues
  • Explored SCADA, GIS, IoT and analytics for digital monitoring
  • Contributed to a hybrid governance/O&M model and helped structure recommendations and the final presentation

    Problem

    Startups and investors operate with limited mutual visibility — founders struggle to be discovered credibly, and investors struggle to verify and track what they're looking at.

    I mapped the problem from both sides — what founders need to be found and trusted, and what investors need to discover and evaluate efficiently — before settling on trust and information flow as the core gap.
